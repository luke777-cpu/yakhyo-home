This shape **can't be recognized from a single dose alone.** Looked at by itself, one peak looks unremarkable. Only when you line up a whole day's peaks does it become visible that they narrow and the troughs widen as the day goes on.

There's an order to reading it. First, check whether **the spacing between the dose marks stays even.** If the spacing itself is uneven, you can't tell whether the held stretch got shorter or the dose was just taken late. Once you've confirmed the spacing is even, look at how far ahead of the next dose each peak starts coming down.

By feel, this becomes "the medication got weaker," but what actually changes on the curve is usually **the width, not the height.** This difference only shows up once you record the time it began to feel heavy again.
