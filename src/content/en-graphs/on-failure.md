The thing to be most careful about when reading this shape is **not mixing up "hasn't risen yet" with "never rose at all."** If you only look at the first hour right after the dose, this shape is indistinguishable from a delayed rise.

That's why you need to look at **the whole stretch after the dose**. Only if the curve stays near the floor all the way until the next dose is marked is this actually the shape in question. In the graph above, the dotted line traces a peak that rises and comes back down, but the solid line stays nearly flat through that entire stretch.

The same principle applies to your records. Writing down only "it didn't work" doesn't let you separate the two shapes later. **Adding one more entry of output right before the next dose** fills in the gap in between, and makes it possible to tell them apart.
