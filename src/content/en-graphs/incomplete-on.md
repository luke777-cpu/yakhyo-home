This is a textbook case of **something that disappears if you only record it as good or bad**. It's not that nothing happened, and it's not that the usual amount happened either, so if you have to pick one of the two, neither one fits well.

On the curve it's easy to see. The solid line starts rising at the same time as the dotted line, but it stops at about half height and comes back down from there. The horizontal direction is similar; only the vertical direction is different.

Recording output by degree carries the most weight for this pattern. **Once a single peak number accumulates over several days**, how many times it was low, and at which dose, stays on record just as it happened.
