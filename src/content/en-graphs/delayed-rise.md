Lay the two curves over each other and the difference is clear. The dotted line and the solid line show doses taken at the same time, but the solid line stays flat at the bottom for a while before it finally starts to rise.

One common misreading here is thinking, **"a day it comes up late is a day the medication was weak."** In fact, there are days when the peak still climbs to about the same height as the dotted line. On days like that, the medication wasn't weak — the start was just delayed — and the two situations call for recording different information.

So for this shape, **if you get just two things right — the dosing time and the time it started to change** — the curve will tell you the rest.
