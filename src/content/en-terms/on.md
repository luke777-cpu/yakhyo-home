ON is not a diagnosis. It is **a name for a stretch of time** — a short way of referring to the period after a dose when the body follows along comparatively well.

On a curve, it is the part that rises after a dose marker and stays high for a while. Drawing a line to say exactly where it starts and ends is difficult, though. Real curves do not switch on and off like a step; they rise and fall gradually.

That is why this diary does not ask you to decide whether you are ON right now. Note **how well you are moving at this moment**, and the higher stretches emerge on their own when you look back at the curve. Moments that feel ambiguous can be recorded exactly as ambiguous.
