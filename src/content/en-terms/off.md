Like ON, OFF is **a name for a stretch of time**. It is not a verdict on your condition — it is a short way of pointing at the stretch where movement feels heavy.

On a curve it is the part that dips low between one dose and the next. Seen across a whole day, as above, what stands out is where those low stretches sit. Whether they always arrive just before the next dose, only in the afternoon, or scattered without regard to the clock differs from person to person.

Writing anything down during a hard stretch is not easy. Even so, **just the time** is worth capturing — it tells you a great deal later. The details can be filled in once things have lifted.
