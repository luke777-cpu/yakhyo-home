Dyskinesia is a difficulty in **the opposite direction** from stiffness. The trouble is not that the body will not move, but that movement you did not intend appears.

The curve itself does not tell you about dyskinesia. The high part of the graph above does not mean dyskinesia. What helps is **marking the times it occurred as events**, so you can see where those marks land on the curve. Once several days accumulate, whether that position is consistent begins to show.

It often passes without the person noticing. If a family member pointed it out, that is worth recording just as it is. The time alone is enough.
