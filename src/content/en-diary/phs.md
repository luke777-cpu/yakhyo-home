Clinic visits are short. **"I've been feeling worse lately" and "the peak after the third dose drops by half, and that happened four days this week" are completely different conversations.** A report is for building the second sentence.

There's one thing worth flagging here. **The numbers in the summary are a value calculated from your records, not a test result.** They don't measure how severe your condition is, and they don't compare you with anyone else. Think of them as a ruler laid across this week and last week for the same person.

So don't adjust your own medication just because a number goes up or down. **The role of your records ends at organizing what to discuss.** What comes next is something to decide together in the visit.

The single most effective thing you can do to prepare is **picking two or three things ahead of time**. If you try to explain everything, the time runs out first, and the thing you actually wanted to ask ends up left out.
