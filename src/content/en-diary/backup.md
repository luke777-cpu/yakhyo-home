It's a basic feature of this app that **your records stay only on the device you use**. Because nothing is sent to a server, there's no risk of it leaking to anyone else — but the flip side is that **if the device is gone, the records are gone too.**

That's why you need to export them out from time to time. Exporting creates a single file, and a backup isn't complete until that file is stored **somewhere off the device**. If you leave it sitting on the device, it disappears along with the device.

The most important moment is **right before you switch devices**. Even if you have a backup from a few months ago, anything logged since then isn't in that file. Just remember to export one more time before you move.

The file contains your dose and symptom records. Keep it in your own email or personal cloud storage, and don't upload it to a folder that several people share.
