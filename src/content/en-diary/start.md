title: Getting Started
description: A step-by-step guide to what to do first when you open the medication diary, and how many days of records it takes before a curve shows up.
lead: You don't need to record everything from the start. Even just noting dose times and the moments your body changes is enough for the shape of the curve to show.
order: 1
steps:
  - title: Register the medications and times you usually take
    detail: Once you enter them, you won't have to type them in again every time. They don't need to be exact — just enter your usual routine for now.
  - title: Log each dose the moment you take it
    detail: If you try to remember and log it later, the minute-by-minute timing gets blurry. This time is the reference point for everything else.
  - title: Record an output whenever you feel your body change
    detail: It helps to log both when you feel better and when you feel heavier. Three or four times a day is enough.
  - title: Mark meals or anything unusual as an event
    detail: Log things like meals, outings, or exercise — anything that might affect the curve.
  - title: After about three days, look at the graph
    detail: A single day's worth doesn't show much. A repeating pattern only shows up once several days have built up.
notes:
  - It's fine if you miss a day. Records don't need to be perfect — once a few days add up, the shape starts to show.
  - If you only log the good moments, you'll only draw the top half of the curve. You need to log the hard moments too, so the ups and downs show.
  - You don't need to decide whether this moment counts as good or bad. Just log about how things are right now.
  - Your records stay on the device you use. This website does not receive any of your records.
faq:
  - q: How many times a day should I record?
    a: There's no set number. Once for every dose, and once whenever you feel your body change, is enough for the shape of the curve to show. Try starting with three or four times a day.
  - q: How many days of records does it take before something shows up?
    a: Three to four days is enough to start telling apart what repeats from what only happened that one day. If you have a clinic visit coming up, about a week's worth is good to have to talk about.
  - q: I missed a few days in the middle — can I just start again?
    a: Yes. Missing some days doesn't reduce the value of the records that come after. You don't need to start over from the beginning — just pick up from where you left off.

The most common worry when you first start is, "What if I don't record it right?" But this record-keeping isn't a test — it's closer to **gathering material to talk about at your next clinic visit**. Even if something is missing, the rest still carries that much value.

When you're starting out, there are just two things to keep to: **logging the time you took your medication, right when you take it**, and **logging the time whenever you feel your body change**. Everything else can be added one step at a time once you're used to it — there's no rush.
