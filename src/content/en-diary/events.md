  - q: What counts as worth logging as an event?

Response and doses alone are enough to draw the curve. Events are the **clue for tracing back why the curve looked the way it did**.

Two kinds matter most. **Meals** — how close a meal was to a dose often connects to the early part of the curve. And **things that pass in a moment, like dyskinesia and freezing of gait** — these don't show up in the curve itself, and only logging them as events lets you see where on the peak they land.

There's no need to feel pressure about this. The goal isn't to record all day long — it's enough to be able to point to what was different on a day that didn't look like usual. Days with nothing to note can stay empty.
